03 FORECAST METHOD

One comparable forecast for every in-scope item.

The output is a gross price forecast, not a promise of executable profit. Markets below 1 Chaos and assets with a persistent decline across broadly covered past leagues remain outside this investment universe, along with the excluded small-consumable categories. Row titles include the exact visible variant identity, including gem state, Forbidden Jewel kind/passive, item level, and links.

  1. 01 Broad-league target

    For each horizon, estimate the future Divine price from exact Medium/High-confidence rows in the official poe.ninja league dumps for Mirage, Keepers, Mercenaries, and Settlers, with newer leagues weighted more heavily. Low-confidence rows stay in the local audit archive but cannot enter the forecast or weighted curve.

  2. 02 Current-curve trend

    Measure the item’s robust current-league log-price trend and cap it so a brief spike or drop cannot dominate the forecast.

  3. 03 70 / 30 log blend

    When the current curve is usable, blend 70% of the historical future price level with 30% of its capped projection in log-price space. Otherwise use the historical target alone.

  4. 04 Horizon ranking

    After the investment-universe filters, rank every remaining exact variant by gross expected gain for the selected 3, 7, or 14-day window. A variant remains listed with a missing forecast when no qualifying future observation exists; friction and liquidity do not alter the order.

DATA SETTINGS

Local market archive

Sync stores poe.ninja current overviews locally across all 18 exchange and 25 item-overview categories configured by the app, then archives exact dated poe.ninja detail histories for the top-ranked identities. Source item IDs and visible variant fields must match; each dated price uses a direct same-day Divine/Chaos observation. Storage is deliberately broader than the investment universe, so excluded low-end consumables, sub-1-chaos items, and persistent past-league decliners remain available for research. GGG’s passive-tree export and build samples are metadata, not alternative price sources. The official Currency Exchange backfill is a separate research archive and cannot enter a forecast curve.

Completed-league prices are imported from the official ZIP dumps at poe.ninja/poe1/data. Exact item IDs and variants must match the current catalog, currency quotes must be directly against Chaos, and the local SQLite checkpoint prevents an unchanged dump from being downloaded again. Legacy poe.watch rows, if present, remain audit-only and can never enter a price curve or forecast score.

Forecasts use only the broadly covered leagues: Mirage, Keepers, Mercenaries, and Settlers. Only poe.ninja rows graded Medium or High (normalized confidence at least 0.5) enter their recency-weighted price levels or the displayed weighted curve. Low rows remain stored locally for audit. The qualifying target is blended 70/30 in log-price space with a capped robust projection of the current-league curve. A missing qualifying future-day observation stays missing. The decline filter uses weekly median Divine-relative curves, robust slopes, and recency-weighted agreement across at least two broad leagues. No friction or liquidity adjustment changes the order. A valid current-versus-past ascendancy share signal adjusts Forbidden Jewel targets only.

Each selected broad-league ZIP is imported once; its normalized daily rows and safe checkpoint stay in local SQLite.

Historical rows require an exact poe.ninja item ID/variant and direct Chaos quote from the same dump. Low-confidence rows remain available for local audit but cannot enter forecasts or the weighted historical curve. Missing dates or Divine anchors remain blank—there is no adjacent-day or cross-provider fallback.
