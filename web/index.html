03 FORECAST METHOD

One comparable forecast for every item.

There are no per-item screening gates. The output is a gross price forecast, not a promise of executable profit. Routine low-value consumable categories and base currencies remain outside this investment universe.

  1. 01 Broad-league target

    For each horizon, estimate the future Divine price from Mirage, Keepers, Mercenaries, and Settlers at the matching league day, with newer leagues weighted more heavily.

  2. 02 Current-curve trend

    Measure the item’s robust current-league log-price trend and cap it so a brief spike or drop cannot dominate the forecast.

  3. 03 70 / 30 log blend

    When the current curve is usable, blend 70% of the historical future price level with 30% of its capped projection in log-price space. Otherwise use the historical target alone.

  4. 04 Horizon ranking

    Rank by gross expected gain for the selected 3, 7, or 14-day window. Friction, liquidity, confidence, falling-knife rules, and structural item vetoes do not alter the order.

DATA SETTINGS

Local market archive

Sync stores supported poe.ninja snapshots and official Currency Exchange aggregates locally across all 18 exchange and 25 item-overview categories configured by the app. Storage is deliberately broader than the investment universe: low-end consumables such as essences, fossils, oils, resonators, scarabs, Delirium Orbs, and routine base currencies remain in the local archive but cannot enter the forecast ranking. Exact Forbidden Jewel prices come from the archived poe.watch catalog, while GGG’s official passive-tree export supplies their ascendancy ownership. The app also samples the official top-experience ladder as archived market context. Seasonal backfill stores exactly matched common markets across six reviewed completed leagues; each pass is resumable and can take several minutes.

Forecasts use only the broadly covered leagues: Mirage, Keepers, Mercenaries, and Settlers. Their future price levels are recency weighted, then blended 70/30 in log-price space with a capped robust projection of the current-league curve. A missing exact future-day observation stays missing. No friction, liquidity, confidence, falling-knife, or structural-item rule changes the forecast or rank.

Uses exact league-day prices from completed softcore trade leagues and locally archived poe.ninja build-composition snapshots.

Divine conversion anchors are sanity-checked. Implausible source days are removed from derived prices while raw responses remain local; a sparse direct outlier also needs two tightly agreeing peer leagues before rejection. A missing day is filled only by a low-confidence same-day median from at least two other validated leagues.
